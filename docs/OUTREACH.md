# QuRoots outreach kit

Goal: 10 paying customers before Ramadan (about 8 Feb 2027).
Rule: one hour of outreach for every hour of coding. Feature freeze until the first sales.

Fill in the [brackets]. Send these yourself, from your own account, in your own voice.

---

## 1. Message to the 32 accounts (the "why didn't you buy" note)

Send one at a time, by email or DM. Do NOT bulk-send. Only message people who made an account and gave you their email.
Goal: learn, not sell. Do not mention the price in this message.

**Subject:** Quick question about QuRoots

> Assalamu alaikum [name],
>
> I'm Alam, the person who built QuRoots. I saw you made an account, thank you.
>
> I'm a student building this on my own, and I'm trying to work out what's missing. Could you tell me in a sentence or two: what were you hoping to get from it, and what made you stop?
>
> Even "it wasn't clear what to do next" is really useful. No sales pitch, I promise.
>
> Jazakallahu khayran,
> Alam

Notes:
- If they reply, answer within the day, then ask one follow-up question.
- Write every reply in the tracker (section 4) word for word. Patterns show up after about 8 replies.
- If someone says "I'd buy but ...", fix that one thing first.

## 2. Pitch to a mosque, MSA or Quran school (the free Ramadan offer)

**Who to contact first (in this order):**
1. UManitoba MSA executives (you are a student there, so this is the easiest start).
2. Weekend Quran school teachers you already know.
3. Imams or youth coordinators at 2-3 Winnipeg mosques.

**Short version for a DM or WhatsApp:**

> Assalamu alaikum [name]. I'm Alam, a UManitoba student. I built QuRoots, a free tool that teaches the Quran word by word through its Arabic roots, so you start to understand what you recite in salah.
>
> For Ramadan I made two free paths: 30 nights following the Taraweeh juz, and a 30-day Juz 'Amma path. Would you be open to sharing them with [your group / the students]? I'd be happy to give a 10-minute walkthrough, and to hear what would make it more useful for them.

**Longer version for email or an in-person chat:**

> Assalamu alaikum [name],
>
> I'm Alam, a computer science and physics student at the University of Manitoba. I've spent the last year building QuRoots (quroots.com), which teaches Quranic Arabic through the roots of its words. The data comes from the Quranic Arabic Corpus, and the sources are listed on the site.
>
> There are three things I think could help [your community]:
> 1. **Free Ramadan paths.** A 30-night path that follows the Taraweeh juz, with about 10 key roots per night. A separate 30-day Juz 'Amma path. Both are free.
> 2. **The Salah path.** A 30-day path that explains what we say in prayer. The first 7 days are free. After that it is a one-time payment of $29, with a 14-day refund.
> 3. **Sponsored places.** If you'd like to support those who can't pay, a place can be gifted and sponsored. Nobody who can't afford it is turned away.
>
> Could I give a 10-minute walkthrough, or send a short message you could share with [students / the community]? I'd really value your honest feedback too.
>
> Jazakallahu khayran,
> Mohammad Alam
> quroots.com

**Do not claim:** that scholars have reviewed the content (until one actually has), or that it replaces a teacher. The site footer already carries an accuracy notice. Say the same thing out loud.

**Ask for one of these, never "please support us":**
- a 10-minute demo,
- sharing in their group or announcements,
- one teacher to try the Ramadan path and tell you what is wrong,
- a sponsor for 5 or 10 places.

## 5. When to change the plan

After about 3 weeks (around 22 Nov), look at the tracker:
- 8+ replies and the same objection each time: fix that one thing.
- Lots of replies, no purchases: the price, the offer or the trust is the problem. Talk to 3 people on a call.
- Few replies: the message or the audience is wrong. Change who you ask, not the product.
- Under 5 messages sent per week: the problem is your outreach volume, not the product.
